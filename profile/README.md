## What we build

Aniseekr is a local-first anime tracker for iOS and Android, with a pilgrimage (聖地巡禮) companion built in.

- **One library, every source** — AniList, MyAnimeList, Bangumi, Kitsu, Annict, Simkl and Shikimori. Sign in to sync your list both ways, or keep it entirely on-device.
- **Pilgrimage, end to end** — scene map, trip planner, a camera that lines your shot up with the original frame, and screenshot-to-scene lookup.
- **Season at a glance** — airing schedule, episode reminders, stats and a year-in-review of what you watched.
- **Yours to keep** — data lives on your phone; optional end-to-end encrypted backup to your own iCloud / Google Drive.
- **Five languages** — English, 繁體中文, 简体中文, 日本語, 한국어.

<sub>iOS 16.4+ · Android 8.0+</sub>
