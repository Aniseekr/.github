## What is Aniseekr?

Aniseekr is a **local-first anime tracker** for iOS and Android with a built-in **pilgrimage (聖地巡礼) camera** — frame the real-world location against the original anime scene, right from your viewfinder.

- 🗂 **One library, every source.** Switch between AniList, Bangumi, MAL, Kitsu, Annict, Simkl and Shikimori without losing your data.
- 📍 **Pilgrimage map.** Cross-indexed Anitabi + Anime Tourism 88 scene coordinates, with a Vision-Camera compare flow.
- 🔒 **Local-first & private.** Your library lives on-device (MMKV + SQLite). Optional **end-to-end encrypted** backups to your own iCloud / Google Drive.
- 🎨 **Themeable.** 8 palettes, custom accents, contrast & tint controls, light/dark/auto.
- 🚫 **No tracking SDKs** on the data path. Apple Privacy Manifest + Play Data Safety ship with every release.
